## Brand & Style

This design system channels an underground cyber-rave counter-intelligence broadcast: an electric, hyper-saturated fusion of early 2000s net art, pirate radio culture, and radical investigative fandom. It operates at the intersection of raw neo-brutalism and vibrant club-flyer maximalism, eschewing muddy dystopian noir in favor of high-exposure, daylight-phosphor illumination. 

Visual characteristics anchor in:
- **Neo-Brutalist Sticker Shock**: Harsh black framing lines (2px–3px), hard non-diffused block shadows, and tape-affixed classified labels.
- **Y2K Rave Futurism**: High-frequency halftones, screaming chromatic accents, scanline patterns, and dynamic running marquee tickers.
- **Propaganda Typography**: Heavy compressed sans-serif headers paired with unyielding terminal monospaces for redactions, leaks, and cryptographic verification logs.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-voltage day-glo phosphors contrasting against a pure stark base and heavy obsidian accents. The environment does not hide in the dark; it weaponizes high-key luminance.

- **Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`)**: Primary operational beacon. Used for key action items, radical confirmations, and active status indicators.
- **Radioactive Hot Pink (`#FF007F`)**: Secondary alert tone. Dominates urgent alerts, unredacted exclusives, and trending conspiracy nodes.
- **Hyper Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: Tertiary cybernetic conduit. Highlights technical logs, data links, audio player meters, and intercepted frequencies.
- **Stark Warning Yellow (`#FFE600`)**: Cautionary flashpoint. Drives marquee warning rails, declassified banners, and threat tier badges.
- **Cyber Lavender (`#D8B4FE`)**: Structural bridge tint. Serves as secondary surface fills, dampening harsh transitions between pure neon and canvas white.
- **Obsidian Ink (`#0F0F14`)**: Foundational structural ink. Replaces standard grays for crisp borders, hard offset shadows, high-contrast typography, and redacted blocks.
- **Ghost White Canvas (`#F8F9FA`)**: Primary background base ensuring maximum color pop without eye-fatigue muddiness.

## Typography

Typography delivers urgent counter-culture dispatching. 

- **Space Grotesk** commands the system’s primary voice: brutalist, geometric, eccentric, and punchy. Headline treatments favor uppercase transforms, tight negative tracking, and intentional text layering over graphic panels.
- **Space Mono** provides bureaucratic authenticity: used across data stamps, timestamps, classification badges, file size metadata, coordinates, and horizontal marquee ribbons.
- Text blocks designated as **REDACTED** use inverted background fills (`#0F0F14`) concealing the underlying text until explicitly hovered or selected by the user.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed using a high-density, rigid 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (switching to 4 columns on mobile, 8 columns on tablet). 

- **Structural Outlines**: The grid structure is intentionally made visible through persistent vertical and horizontal 1px hairline dividers (`#0F0F14`).
- **Dense Packing**: Information density is pushed to simulate tactical dispatch dashboards. Negative space is functional, preventing visual collision between neon fields, but components do not float ambiguously.
- **Marquee Rails**: The top and bottom viewport edges feature locked, continuous ticker lines that clip content cleanly and dictate structural margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft, ambient blur shadows in favor of hard-edged, neo-brutalist pop drop-shadows and tactile paper-and-tape elevation.

- **Hard Block Shadows**: Elevation is created using zero-blur offset projections:
  - Tier 1 (Cards, Badges): `3px 3px 0px #0F0F14`
  - Tier 2 (Hover states, Modals): `6px 6px 0px #0F0F14`
  - Tier 3 (Floating Action Stamps): `8px 8px 0px #0F0F14`
- **Neon Underglow Borders**: Interactive elements combine the solid 2px black outline with an adjacent, high-luminance hard rim: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0F0F14, 0 0 12px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Halftone & Screentone Overlays**: Secondary elevation tiers use subtle black halftone dots (10%–15% opacity) over colored backgrounds to evoke underground zine printing methods.

## Shapes

The design system enforces strict geometric angularity (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Corners are 100% sharp to maintain high-impact cyber-brutalist discipline.
- Diagonal corner cuts (chamfered edges at 45 degrees, 8px–12px) are applied to identity cards, badge containers, and warning banners.
- Tape tabs and adhesive sticker overlays feature intentionally imperfect, slightly rotated bounding boxes (-1.5 deg to +2 deg) pinned directly across container corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Rave Button**: Fills with Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`) or Radioactive Hot Pink (`#FF007F`), encapsulated by a 2.5px solid Obsidian (`#0F0F14`) border. Features a hard `4px 4px 0px #0F0F14` shadow. On hover, translates 2px down and right with shadow reducing to `2px 2px 0px`, accompanied by an electric chromatic text jitter.
- **Terminal Ghost Button**: Transparent background, 2px `#0F0F14` border, monospace text. Inverts immediately to solid Obsidian with neon text on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Classified Stamp**: Monospaced all-caps text framed in a 1.5px dashed border. Rotated slightly (-2° to 3°).
- **Status Indicator**: Solid Acid Lime or Cyber Cyan pill with a blinking phosphor square dot (`animation: pulse 1s steps(2, start) infinite`).

### Cards & News Feeds
- **Truth Dossier Card**: Ghost White background, 2.5px solid border, `4px 4px 0px #0F0F14` offset shadow. Top header bar filled with Warning Yellow (`#FFE600`) or Cyber Lavender (`#D8B4FE`) separating title from dispatch content. Media items feature a high-contrast halftone duotone filter that shifts to full color on cursor entry.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Data Terminal Inputs**: Heavy 2px borders, sharp rectangular forms, `#F8F9FA` background shifting to `#FFFFFF` on focus, paired with an intense Acid Lime glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #CCFF00`). Placeholders appear in strict bracketed monospace: `[ENTER_PASSCODE]`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square-only checkboxes with sharp corners. Selected state renders as a high-voltage hot pink block containing an obsidian "X".

### Propaganda Additions
- **Continuous Marquee Banner**: Edge-to-edge high-velocity running warning banner with alternating Warning Yellow and Obsidian diagonal hazard stripes.
- **Redaction Reveal Bars**: Obsidian blocks masking classified intelligence; hovering dissolves the black block into a cyber cyan highlight reveal.
